Is this result fit to release?

Enter a glucose result and what happened to it. The system decides whether to release it, re-run the same sample, or take fresh blood — and tells you which, because the family the fault belongs to decides whether the patient has to be bled again.

Two examples — same number, different answer

How it works. The knowledge base is ordinary Prolog running in your browser through Tau Prolog. Nothing is sent anywhere — no server, no network, no storage. Every decision on this page is made by a Prolog rule; JavaScript only collects the form and displays the answer.

What it decides. Whether a glucose result is fit to leave the laboratory, and what to do about it if it is not. It does not interpret what a result means for a patient. That is the clinician’s work.

Status. Every threshold and fault rule is a draft from textbooks and papers, not yet confirmed by the domain expert. Knowledge source in use: —.
