How it works. The knowledge base is ordinary Prolog running in your browser through Tau Prolog. Nothing is sent anywhere. Forward chaining derives the faults from what you enter; backward chaining then proves which action follows. Every question, every verdict and every reason on this page comes from a rule in kb/. The interface decides nothing.

Why it stops early. Questions are ordered by the best action they could establish. Once a fault is found, everything still unasked could only produce a lower-ranked action, so the system stops. Open Why on any result to see how many questions it skipped and on what grounds.

Status. Every threshold and fault rule is a draft awaiting the domain expert’s confirmation. Knowledge source in use: not loaded yet.
